title: About Yifan Zhang
---

I'm a Digital Media Technology student at Beijing University of Technology,
and a beginner stepping into AIGC. This site is the room where I keep what
I've made so far — scripts, character studies, storyboards, workflow notes,
and the short films themselves — kept whole, exactly as the process left
them.

What I'm working toward is one short film that is unmistakably mine — a
piece that says what I would say if I had the language for it. Right now
both the creative instinct and the technical grip on how to *steer* the
generators are still at their first light. The works in this archive are
the shape that light is taking, frame by frame.

I'm here to learn. If anything you see is interesting, reach out — every
conversation, every collaboration, every chance I get to actually make
something, I'll take seriously.
