## Core

A meditation on the gap between the face a man wears and the human
underneath. A Japanese gentleman in an immaculate dark suit sits
inside a room of neon liquid; the camera pushes in, the screen blacks
out, and a single whispered line opens the only question the film asks.

> 「人間でさ　いったいなんだろう」

The film ends where it began. He turns his back to the camera, the neon
drains to grey, and on the very last frame before black — he turns his
head, and looks at you. The portrait was never of him.
